Sum up 20 November's world news in one image: your program writes one image of the day's news, an AI judge compares the images two at a time, and the 50 best win rates share $250: $50 for 1st place, $30 for 2nd, $15 for 3rd, $5 each for 4th to 10th and $3 each for 11th to 50th.

A picture-editor style infographic of Friday 20 November 2026, drawn entirely in code (pure Python 3.13 standard library — no images downloaded, no third-party packages). It names the day and sums up its six biggest stories: Russia's overnight missile and drone attack on Kyiv, which killed three and damaged three bridges across the Dnipro; Saudi air defences intercepting a Houthi ballistic missile and drones a day after the PIF tower in Riyadh was struck; the AI sell-off sinking Asian stocks, with the Nikkei down 4.8% and SoftBank down 16%; tens of thousands of young people striking across more than 25 European countries over living costs and militarisation; GTA 6's first-day sales put at $2.36bn, the biggest entertainment launch ever; and the IMF warning that global public debt is heading for 100% of world GDP — plus briefs on Anthropic's after-hours fall and Argentina's Senate backing a permanent disability framework.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```

This writes `out/entry.png` (1024×1024 PNG, deterministic — two runs produce byte-identical output).
